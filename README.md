# Campus Connect

A Quest Board for students: find guides, teammates, and opportunities through structured cards instead of a social feed.

Built for Nova Challenge #02.

🔗 Live prototype: [paste your Vercel link here]

## The problem

I wasted a year trying to learn tech because I couldn't find the right person to guide me. Students lose time not from lack of effort, but because opportunities, mentors, and teammates are scattered across group chats, noticeboards, and social feeds.

## The solution

Campus Connect replaces the feed with cards. Every post has a type, a deadline, and one action button, so students act instead of scroll.

- Guide Match: find someone who has walked the path
- Squad Builder: post a project and the roles you need
- Skill Swap: "I teach CSS, I want to learn Python"
- Opportunity Radar: hackathons and internships by deadline

## What the prototype includes

- Quest Board with 6 colour-coded cards (Guide, Squad, Opportunity, Skill Swap)
- Card buttons that respond when clicked
- "Post a quest" form with validation (title length, required fields, no past deadlines)
- Squad page with members, open slots, check-ins, and days left
- Responsive layout for phones and laptops

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks.

## What I'd build next

Real accounts and matching, deadline notifications, and a verified-guide badge so students trust who they're learning from.
